## Narration and picture plan

Each paragraph is an editorial section, not a requirement to generate one long clip. Split into sentence-sized presenter clips and interleave the indicated screen recordings. Keep an unbroken narration track under B-roll where possible.

### 1. Meet Ava and choose your path

**Picture:** Ava in the reference outfit, medium shot; subtle push-in, then the actual homepage and Studio entry.

I'm Ava Skye, your AI assistant inside DigiMark101. If marketing feels like a collection of disconnected tools, let me show you a more practical place to start. You bring the business, the idea, and the decisions. I help turn those decisions into organized work, useful drafts, and a clearer next step.

There are two paths here. You can use Studio, the marketing workspace, with my help inside the app. Or you can ask DigiMark101 about agency services when you want a scoped project and hands-on help. A workspace tool and an agency engagement are different things, and I will explain that difference rather than promise that a subscription includes every service.

### 2. Type or speak inside the app

**Picture:** Enter Studio, open Ava, type a request, then demonstrate the microphone permission and a spoken request. Show the actual transcript; include the Stop control. Do not show a floating widget on the public landing page.

Inside Studio, you can type to me or use voice on a supported device. Start with ordinary language: tell me what you want to achieve, who you want to reach, and what you already have. You do not need to know the name of every screen before asking for help.

For example, ask me to draft a welcome email for a new customer, organize a sales opportunity, or help plan your first offer. For voice, allow microphone access when your browser asks, speak clearly, and check the words that appear. If the device cannot use voice, typing remains available. You can stop listening or stop my spoken reply whenever you need to.

### 3. See the work, not just a chat reply

**Picture:** Record each request on a demonstration account, including its actual result. Use readable close-ups, not invented animations of a successful save.

Let us start with a contact. You can ask me to add a person with the details you provide, and then check that person's record in your workspace. That organizes the information; it does not automatically email or text them. If an important detail is missing or the request is ambiguous, we need to resolve it rather than guess.

Next, ask me to create a sales opportunity and place it in the appropriate stage. The pipeline helps you see what needs attention and move a deal as the conversation progresses. A deal changing stage is an organizational action, not proof that money has arrived.

Now ask for a campaign draft. Give me the audience, the offer, and the tone you want. I can help prepare the words and save supported drafts, and you can review the result on screen. Saving a draft is not the same as sending it. Sending, publishing, spending, or destructive changes need the appropriate access and confirmations, and some delivery connections are still being built.

### 4. Build a more organized marketing workspace

**Picture:** Actual Contacts, Pipeline, Calendar, Sites, Forms, Campaigns, Social, Files, and Apps screens; show draft/status labels at a readable size.

Studio brings contacts, sales opportunities, calendar records, page and form drafts, campaign planning, and files into one workspace. You can work one piece at a time instead of trying to launch an entire business in a single conversation. Start with one offer, one audience, and one useful task.

For a first campaign, that might mean planning a landing page, preparing a form, and drafting a short follow-up sequence. The objective is a reviewable starting point, not a claim that every connection is already live. A draft site record is not a published website, and a calendar record is not automatically a synchronized external calendar.

When a longer draft needs help, six helpers support pages and offers, social posts, ad notes, search outlines, follow-up, and research. I remain the assistant you speak with. The apps catalog shows connection status, and the video maker can prepare scripts and storyboards; finished presenter video requires configured rendering services and a separate quality review.

### 5. Explore the agency services

**Picture:** Real service pages. Identify each as an agency offering, not an included automated action. No stock client logos, invented charts, or fabricated testimonials.

If you want agency help, DigiMark101 offers website design, search engine optimization, ads management, social media, content, email, and analytics services. The right starting point depends on your business and the problem you are trying to solve. Fees, responsibilities, and deliverables belong in a proposal, not in a vague promise that everything is included.

A website project can focus on explaining your offer and helping visitors take the next step. Search work can examine how your pages answer relevant questions. Content and social planning help organize what you want to communicate. Email work can shape a useful follow-up sequence, while ads management and analytics require a clear goal, the right connections, and an agreed scope.

You can ask for help choosing a direction before committing to a larger project. We should establish what is needed, what access is required, what will be delivered, and how the work will be reviewed. I will not invent rankings, customer results, or a guaranteed return to make the offer sound stronger.

### 6. Know what is ready and stay in control

**Picture:** Real draft badges, permission/confirmation flows, and a failed or unavailable action handled honestly. Only demonstrate a confirmation for an operation that actually requires it.

My job is to help you do supported work, not to tell you that an action succeeded when it did not. Read the result, inspect the saved record, and check the status before assuming something is live. If a provider, permission, or integration is missing, that limitation matters.

Email and text delivery, social publishing, payments, and external connections should only be described as live after they have been verified in the environment you are using. An invoice record does not charge a card. A queued message does not prove delivery. No tool can guarantee income; outcomes depend on your offer, effort, audience, and execution.

### 7. Take the next step

**Picture:** Return to Ava, then show current account, Studio, and contact links from the real site. Validate current signup terms before filming; do not bake an expiring offer into the narration.

You do not have to solve everything today. Choose one goal, create your account or explore the available Studio preview, and bring me one task. Type it or speak it inside the app. We can start with a contact, a sales opportunity, or a useful draft, then review what actually happened before moving on.

If you prefer agency help, use the contact path and tell us what you want to build. I am Ava Skye. Bring your ideas, keep control of your decisions, and let us take the next practical step together.
